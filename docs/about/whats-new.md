## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we made some significant changes to our documentation that I’m excited to share with you. We decided to remove the "What's New" section from our documentation. This change was made to streamline our content and make it easier for you to find the information that truly matters. Instead of a separate section, we’re focusing on integrating updates directly into relevant areas of the documentation, ensuring that you have the most current information at your fingertips without having to navigate to a different page. You can read more about this decision [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

I believe this shift is important because it reflects our commitment to enhancing your experience as you navigate through our resources. By consolidating updates, we aim to reduce clutter and make your journey through our documentation more intuitive. This means you can spend less time searching and more time focusing on what you need to achieve with the Fabric Adoption Framework. I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to everyone who contributed to this decision and the ongoing improvements in our documentation. Your feedback is invaluable to us!

As we move forward, I’m hopeful that these changes will foster a more engaging and user-friendly environment for all of you. I can’t wait to see how this impacts your experience and to hear your thoughts on the new layout. Thank you for being a vital part of our community!
