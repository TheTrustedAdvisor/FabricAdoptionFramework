## August 2025

Hello everyone! I’m excited to share some updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve made significant strides in refining our documentation. Notably, we’ve added a new License section to our documentation, which can now be easily accessed via the navigation menu. This change is important because it ensures that you have all the information you need about how to use our resources responsibly and in accordance with our guidelines (Read more ›).

In addition, we’ve worked hard to synchronize our published strategy methodology documents and expand the visibility of our strategy category within the documentation. This means that you’ll find it easier to navigate through the strategies that can help you with Fabric adoption. We aim to make your journey smoother and more intuitive, allowing you to focus on what truly matters—implementing effective strategies in your work (Read more ›).

Lastly, we’ve made some adjustments to our sidebar categories for clarity, ensuring that you can find what you need without any hassle. I want to thank everyone who contributed to these improvements; your input is invaluable in making our documentation better for all. I’m looking forward to seeing how these changes impact your experience!
