## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! August has been a bustling month for us at the Fabric Adoption Framework, and I’m excited to share some of the wonderful updates we’ve made to enhance your experience. One of the most significant changes is the addition of a new License section to our documentation, which you can now easily access from the navigation menu. This addition not only clarifies the terms of use for our resources but also fosters transparency and trust within our community (Read more ›).

We’ve also worked on synchronizing our published strategy methodology documents, ensuring that you have the most up-to-date information at your fingertips. Alongside this, we expanded the visibility of our strategy category in the documentation, making it easier for you to find relevant content that supports your adoption journey. These changes are all about creating a smoother navigation experience, so you can focus more on what truly matters—your Fabric adoption strategy (Read more ›).

Lastly, we’ve made some updates to our sidebar categories to improve clarity and removed an outdated article that no longer aligns with our current focus. Thank you to everyone who contributed to these changes; your input is invaluable in shaping our community. I hope these enhancements make your journey with us even more enjoyable!
