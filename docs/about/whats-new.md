## June 2025

Hello everyone! I’m excited to share some important updates from June that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we made a significant decision to remove the "What's New" documentation section. While it was a space for sharing updates, we felt that consolidating our information would provide a clearer and more streamlined navigation experience for you. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This change matters because it allows us to focus on delivering content that is more relevant and easier to access. By decluttering our documentation, we hope to make it simpler for you to find what you need without sifting through multiple sections. I believe this will lead to a more intuitive user experience, making your journey through our resources smoother and more enjoyable.

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to everyone who contributed to these changes. Your feedback and support are invaluable as we strive to create a better experience for our community. I’m looking forward to seeing how these updates positively impact your interactions with the framework. If you’re curious about all the changes we made this month, feel free to check out the details [here](https://github.com/TheTrustedAdvisor/FabricAdoptionFramework/commits/main?since=2025-06-01&until=2025-06-30).
