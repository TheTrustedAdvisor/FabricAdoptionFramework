# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! August has been a transformative month for the Fabric Adoption Framework, and I’m excited to share some of the changes we’ve made to enhance your experience. One of the most notable updates is the addition of a dedicated License section in our documentation, which clarifies how you can use our resources (Read more ›). This is important because it not only fosters transparency but also helps you navigate the legal aspects of our framework with ease. We also added a License link to the navigation menu, making it even easier for you to find this information whenever you need it (Read more ›).

In our ongoing effort to improve clarity and accessibility, we’ve collapsed sidebar categories for a cleaner look, allowing you to focus on the content that matters most without feeling overwhelmed (Read more ›). Additionally, we’ve synchronized our published strategy methodology documents and expanded the visibility of strategy categories within the documentation. These changes are designed to help you find the strategies you need more intuitively (Read more ›). I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to all our contributors and the community for your continuous support and feedback, which make these enhancements possible.

As we move forward, I believe these updates will create a more streamlined and user-friendly experience for everyone engaging with the Fabric Adoption Framework. Your journey with us is important, and we’re committed to making it as smooth as possible. Let’s keep building together!
