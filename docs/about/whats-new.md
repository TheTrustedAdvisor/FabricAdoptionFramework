# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some heartwarming updates from the Fabric Adoption Framework this August. We’ve been busy refining our documentation to make your experience smoother and more intuitive. One of the significant changes is the addition of a License section, which now has its own dedicated link in the navigation menu. This change not only clarifies the usage rights but also enhances your ability to find important information quickly (Read more ›).

We’ve also taken steps to improve the overall structure of our documentation by collapsing sidebar categories for clarity. This means you can navigate through our content with greater ease and focus on what truly matters to you. Additionally, we’ve synchronized our published strategies for Fabric adoption, ensuring that you have access to the latest methodologies and insights that can aid your journey (Read more ›). 

I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to all our contributors and the community for your ongoing support and feedback. Your input truly shapes the way we evolve, and I can’t wait to see how these updates will impact your experience with the framework. Together, we’re building a more accessible and engaging resource for everyone involved in Fabric adoption (Read more ›). 

As always, I invite you to explore these changes and let us know what you think! (531 commits)

## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we made a significant change to our Fabric Adoption Framework documentation that I want to share with you. We decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section entirely. This shift is aimed at streamlining our content and making it easier for you to find the information you need without sifting through updates that may not be as relevant to your journey. You can read more about this decision [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

I believe this change matters because it reflects our commitment to providing a more user-friendly experience. By simplifying our navigation and content structure, we hope to enhance your ability to engage with the framework effectively. It’s all about making your experience smoother and more intuitive, so you can focus on what truly matters: adopting the fabric in your projects.

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to the contributors and the community for their input and support during this transition. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us create a better resource for everyone. I’m excited to see how these changes will positively impact your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework!
