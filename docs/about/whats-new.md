## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we've made some significant updates to our documentation that I believe will enhance your experience navigating the Fabric Adoption Framework. One of the most notable changes is the removal of the "What's New" documentation section. While it served its purpose, we felt it was time to streamline our content and make room for more engaging and relevant resources for you. You can read more about this decision [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This change reflects our commitment to continuously improving your journey with us, ensuring that our resources are not only informative but also user-friendly. I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to the contributors and community members who provided valuable feedback that guided this decision.

As we move forward, we expect these adjustments to foster a more seamless interaction with our documentation, empowering you to focus on what truly matters—adopting and implementing the Fabric framework effectively. I'm excited for you to explore the updated content and see how it enhances your experience!
