# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates from August 2025 that enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. We’ve made several meaningful changes to our documentation, starting with the addition of a new License section. This not only clarifies how you can use our resources but also adds a License link to the navigation menu, making it easier for you to find this important information (Read more ›).

In our ongoing effort to improve clarity, we’ve collapsed sidebar categories to streamline navigation. This change is designed to help you find what you need more quickly and intuitively. Additionally, we've synchronized our published strategy methodology documents and expanded the visibility of strategy categories within the docs, ensuring that you have access to the most relevant and up-to-date information (Read more ›). 

I want to take a moment to thank all our contributors and the community for your invaluable input and support. Your feedback helps us create a more user-friendly experience, and I’m thrilled to see how these updates will positively impact your journey with the Fabric Adoption Framework. ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we've made a significant change to our documentation structure that I believe will enhance your experience. We decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section. While it served its purpose, we felt that consolidating our updates into a more streamlined format would make it easier for you to find the information you need without sifting through multiple sections. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This decision matters because it reflects our commitment to providing you with a clearer and more efficient navigation experience. By focusing on essential content and reducing redundancy, we hope to create a more cohesive journey through our documentation. It’s all about making your learning process smoother and more enjoyable, and I genuinely believe this change will contribute positively to that goal.

I want to take a moment to thank our contributors and the community for their ongoing support and feedback. Your insights are invaluable as we strive to improve our documentation continuously. I’m excited about the impact these changes will have, and I hope you are too!
