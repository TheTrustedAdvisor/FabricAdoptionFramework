Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve made significant strides in organizing our content and improving navigation. We’ve collapsed sidebar categories for clarity, making it easier for you to find what you need without feeling overwhelmed (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md). I want to take a moment to thank our contributors and the community for your invaluable feedback and support. Your insights help us create a more user-friendly experience, and I’m looking forward to seeing how these updates positively impact your work. ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made some significant changes to the Fabric Adoption Framework documentation that I’m excited to share with you. Most notably, we decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section entirely. This change was driven by our desire to streamline the content and make it easier for you to find the information you need without sifting through updates that may not be as relevant to your journey. You can read more about this decision [here](https://github.com/TheTrustedAdvisor/FabricAdoptionFramework/commits/main?since=2025-06-01&until=2025-06-30).

I truly believe that this shift will enhance your experience on the site. By focusing on more essential content and eliminating redundancy, we hope to create a more intuitive navigation experience. This means you can spend less time searching and more time engaging with the resources that matter most to you. I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and the community for their feedback and support throughout this process. Your insights are invaluable in helping us create a more user-friendly environment.

As we continue to evolve, I’m optimistic that these changes will have a positive impact on how you interact with our documentation.
