## June 2025

Hello dear readers! This month, we made a significant change to our documentation by removing the "What's New" section. This decision was made to streamline our content and ensure that you can find the most relevant information without any clutter. We believe that by focusing on the core resources that truly matter to you, we can enhance your overall experience on the Fabric Adoption Framework website. If you're curious about the details behind this change, you can read more about it [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

We understand that changes like this can feel a bit disorienting at first, but we truly believe that this will lead to a more cohesive and user-friendly navigation experience. By eliminating the "What's New" section, we hope to encourage you to engage more deeply with the content that is essential for your journey in adopting our framework. Your feedback has always been invaluable, and we appreciate your understanding as we continue to evolve our documentation to better serve you.

Thank you for being part of our community and for your ongoing support. We are excited about the path ahead and are committed to making your experience as enriching as possible. Please feel free to share your thoughts with us as we continue to grow together!
