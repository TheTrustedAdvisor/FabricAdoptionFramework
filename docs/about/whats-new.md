Hello, dear readers! I'm excited to share some updates from August 2025 that I think you'll find quite refreshing. We’ve been working hard to enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework, and I’m thrilled to announce that we’ve added a new License section to our documentation. This addition not only clarifies the usage rights but also ensures that you have all the necessary information at your fingertips (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-2fcc4ca958e093e082d40a93ab14ba804b31822d.md).

This small change is designed to make it easier for you to find important information without having to dig through the content (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-5c7af7d42f049ba974347b811dbea587eedf7a00.md). This means you can now access the strategies more intuitively, helping you find the guidance you need faster (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-9993fe2ef400b1868a1874a379e6fe864b68fd0c.md).

Lastly, we’ve made some adjustments to the sidebar categories for clarity, collapsing them to streamline your browsing experience (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md). I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and community for their invaluable input and support. These changes are all about making your journey with us smoother and more enjoyable. Let's keep moving forward together! (531 commits)

## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we made a significant change to our documentation structure that I believe will enhance your experience. We decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section. While it served its purpose for a time, we felt that consolidating information in a more streamlined manner would better serve your needs. This change allows us to focus on providing you with the most relevant and up-to-date content without the distraction of a separate section dedicated to updates. You can read more about this decision [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

Why does this matter? By simplifying our navigation and content structure, we aim to create a more intuitive experience for you. We want you to find the information you need quickly and efficiently, without having to sift through multiple sections. This change reflects our commitment to continuous improvement and responsiveness to your feedback. Thank you to everyone who contributed to this decision; your insights are invaluable to us.

As we move forward, we expect that this streamlined approach will not only make our documentation easier to navigate but also foster a more engaging and cohesive reading experience. I’m excited to see how this impacts your journey with the Fabric Adoption Framework and look forward to hearing your thoughts!
