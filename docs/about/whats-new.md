Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve made significant strides in both content and navigation. We’ve synchronized new content, including some fresh strategy methodology documents, which will provide you with clearer guidance as you navigate your adoption journey (Read more ›). Additionally, we’ve expanded the visibility of our strategy categories in the documentation, making it easier for you to find the resources you need (Read more ›).

These changes reflect our commitment to continuously improving your experience and making our resources more accessible.

I want to take a moment to thank our contributors and the community for their invaluable input and support. Your feedback helps us shape the direction of our documentation, and we’re thrilled to implement these enhancements that we hope will empower you on your journey. As always, we’re here to support you, and we can’t wait to see how these updates impact your experience! ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some significant updates from June 2025 that I believe will enhance your experience on the Fabric Adoption Framework website. This month, we made the decision to streamline our content by removing the "What's New" documentation section. While it has served us well, we felt that consolidating information would make it easier for you to find what you need without unnecessary clutter. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This change matters because it reflects our commitment to creating a more user-friendly environment. We believe that a cleaner structure will lead to a better overall experience for everyone, and we’re eager to see how this impacts your journey with us.
