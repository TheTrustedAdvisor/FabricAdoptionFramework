## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we made some significant changes to our documentation that I’m excited to share with you. Most notably, we decided to remove the "What's New" section from our site. While this might feel like a big shift, we believe it will streamline your experience and make it easier for you to find the information you need without sifting through updates that may not be relevant to your journey. You can read more about this decision and its implications in detail [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This change matters because it reflects our commitment to enhancing your navigation experience. We understand that your time is precious, and we want to ensure that every moment spent on our site is productive and meaningful. 

I want to take a moment to thank our contributors and the community for their valuable feedback that guided us to this decision. Your insights are invaluable, and we’re hopeful that these adjustments will foster a more engaging and user-friendly environment for everyone. As we continue to evolve, we look forward to hearing your thoughts and experiences. Together, we can make this journey even better!
