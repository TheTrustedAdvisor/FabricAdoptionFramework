# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some lovely updates from August that are all about enhancing your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. We’ve made some significant strides in our documentation, including the addition of a new License section, which now has its own link in the navigation menu. This change not only clarifies the usage rights for our materials but also makes it easier for you to find essential information at a glance (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-2fcc4ca958e093e082d40a93ab14ba804b31822d.md).

We also took a moment to refresh our content and structure, collapsing sidebar categories for better clarity. This means you can navigate through our resources more smoothly, allowing you to focus on what truly matters—your journey in adopting Fabric (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md). Additionally, we’ve synchronized our published strategies for Fabric adoption and expanded the visibility of strategy categories in our documentation, ensuring you have access to the most relevant and helpful guidance (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-9993fe2ef400b1868a1874a379e6fe864b68fd0c.md).

These updates are a result of the hard work and dedication from our contributors and community, and I’m incredibly grateful for your support. I believe these enhancements will empower you to navigate our resources with greater ease and confidence. Thank you for being a part of this journey with us! ## July 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m thrilled to share some exciting updates from July 2025 that I believe will greatly enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This decluttering effort means that you’ll find a more cohesive and engaging experience as you dive into our materials. Overall, these updates are not just about new content; they represent our commitment to creating a supportive and informative environment for everyone involved in the Fabric community. I’m excited to see how these changes will positively impact your journey with us. Thank you for being a part of this community, and I look forward to sharing more updates with you soon! ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made some significant changes that I believe will enhance your experience on the Fabric Adoption Framework website. We decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section entirely. While it served a purpose, we felt that consolidating our content would create a more streamlined navigation experience for you, making it easier to find the information you need without unnecessary clutter (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This change matters because it reflects our commitment to continuously improving how we present information. By simplifying our structure, we hope to make your journey through the documentation smoother and more intuitive. We want you to focus on the content that truly matters without sifting through sections that may no longer serve you well (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-3a35144aa92198574f983f6a3054dd2b5db3ab9c.md).

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to everyone in our community for your feedback and support as we navigate these changes together. Your insights drive us to make the documentation better for all of us, and I’m excited about the positive impact these updates will have on your experience. Let's continue this journey of growth and improvement together!
