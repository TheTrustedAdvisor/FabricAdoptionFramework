## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made a significant change by removing the "What's New" documentation section from our site. While it might seem a bit surprising, this decision was made to streamline our content and enhance your overall experience. By consolidating information, we hope to make it easier for you to find what you need without sifting through multiple sections that may not add as much value. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This adjustment matters because it reflects our commitment to providing you with the most relevant and user-friendly documentation. We believe that by simplifying our structure, you’ll navigate our resources more effortlessly and focus on what truly matters—adopting and implementing our framework effectively. Thank you to everyone who contributed to this decision; your feedback is invaluable in shaping our documentation.

As we move forward, we expect this change will lead to a more cohesive experience for all our users. We’re excited to see how this will help you engage with our content more meaningfully. If you have any thoughts or suggestions on this update, please don’t hesitate to reach out. Together, we can continue to improve and grow!
