# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework documentation. This month, we’ve made significant strides in improving clarity and accessibility. For instance, we’ve collapsed sidebar categories to streamline navigation, making it easier for you to find what you need without feeling overwhelmed (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md). 

Additionally, we’ve added a dedicated License section to our documentation, along with a link in the navigation menu. This change not only keeps everything organized but also ensures that you have quick access to important licensing information (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-2fcc4ca958e093e082d40a93ab14ba804b31822d.md). These updates are part of our ongoing commitment to make the documentation more user-friendly and transparent, and I truly hope they make your journey smoother.

I want to take a moment to thank our contributors and the community for their invaluable input and support. Your feedback helps us shape a better resource for everyone. As we continue to sync and expand our content, I’m looking forward to seeing how these changes positively impact your experience with the framework. ## July 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some significant updates that have taken place in July 2025 for the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve laid the groundwork for our adoption framework by adding initial content that aims to guide you through the adoption process seamlessly. These additions are designed to offer clarity and support as you navigate the framework, making it easier for you to understand and implement. You can dive into the details of this new content [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-07-20-b6ea8bd71edcd6fcab2d774df9ea7b7b415bcbc2.md).

In our effort to streamline the information available to you, we also removed some initial documentation files that were no longer relevant. This helps us create a more focused and user-friendly experience, ensuring that you have access to the most pertinent resources without any clutter. I believe this change will significantly enhance your journey with the framework, allowing for a smoother transition into adoption. For more on these removals, check out this link [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-07-20-3948fa7bc9ab671af8690e6527e831adebbec1dc.md).

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to everyone who contributed to these changes. Your efforts are invaluable in shaping a resource that truly meets the needs of our community. I’m looking forward to seeing how these updates will positively impact your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. Together, we’re building something great! ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we made some significant changes to our documentation that I’m excited to share with you. We decided to remove the "What's New" section from our documentation entirely. This might seem like a big shift, but it’s all about streamlining your experience as you navigate through our resources. By consolidating information, we aim to make it easier for you to find what you need without sifting through multiple sections. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

Why does this matter? Well, clarity and ease of use are at the heart of what we do. We want you to feel at home when you visit our site, and by simplifying our structure, we hope to enhance your overall experience. This change is a step towards creating a more cohesive and user-friendly environment, ensuring that you can focus on what truly matters: adopting and implementing the Fabric framework effectively.

I want to extend a heartfelt thanks to everyone who contributed to this decision and the subsequent adjustments. Your feedback and support are invaluable in shaping our documentation to better serve our community. I’m looking forward to seeing how these changes positively impact your journey with us!
