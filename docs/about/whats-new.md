## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made some significant changes to our documentation structure that I believe will enhance your experience. We decided to remove the "What's New" section from the documentation. While it served its purpose for a time, we felt that it was no longer the best way to communicate updates and changes. Instead, we’re focusing on integrating updates more seamlessly throughout the site, allowing you to discover new content as you navigate. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This shift matters because it streamlines your journey through our documentation. By embedding updates directly within the relevant sections, we hope to create a more cohesive and intuitive user experience. We believe that this approach will help you find what you need more easily and stay informed about the latest developments without having to check a separate section. 

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and the community for their ongoing support and feedback, which has guided us through this decision. We’re excited about the impact these changes will have on your experience, and we look forward to hearing your thoughts as you explore the updated site!
