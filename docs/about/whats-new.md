## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we made some significant changes to our documentation that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. Most notably, we've decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section. This change stems from a desire to streamline our content and make it easier for you to find the information you need without sifting through updates that may no longer serve you. You can read more about this decision [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

Why does this matter? By eliminating the "What's New" section, we aim to create a more cohesive and user-friendly navigation experience. This will allow you to focus on the core content that truly matters to your journey with the framework, rather than getting caught up in past updates. We believe this will lead to a more intuitive understanding of our resources and ultimately empower you to adopt the framework more effectively.

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and community for their ongoing support and feedback. Your insights are invaluable in shaping our documentation to better serve you. I’m excited to see how these changes will positively impact your experience, and I look forward to hearing your thoughts!
