## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates we’ve made to the Fabric Adoption Framework this August. We’ve been busy enhancing your experience, starting with a fresh new look for our logo, which now reflects the vibrant spirit of our community (Read more ›). Additionally, we’ve streamlined our documentation by collapsing sidebar categories for clarity, making it easier for you to navigate and find what you need (Read more ›).

These updates are all about making your journey smoother and more enjoyable as you explore the Fabric Adoption Framework. I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to all our contributors and the community for your ongoing support and feedback. Your input is invaluable, and we’re thrilled to continue evolving together. ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we've made some significant updates to our documentation that I believe will enhance your experience. In a move to streamline our content, we've decided to remove the "What's New" section from the documentation. While this might seem like a big change, it reflects our commitment to keeping the information clear and easily navigable. By focusing on integrating updates directly into relevant sections, we hope to make it simpler for you to find what you need without sifting through separate announcements. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This decision was not made lightly, and I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and community members for their input. Your feedback is invaluable, and it has guided us toward creating a more cohesive experience. By eliminating redundancy and focusing on integrating updates into the main content, we expect to foster a more intuitive navigation experience for everyone. 

As we continue to evolve, I am excited about the positive impact these changes will have on how you interact with our documentation. I truly believe that these adjustments will help you find the information you need more quickly and efficiently. Thank you for being part of our journey, and I look forward to hearing your thoughts on these updates!
