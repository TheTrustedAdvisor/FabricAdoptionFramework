# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates from August 2025 that will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve made significant strides in our documentation, including the addition of a new License section, which you can now find in our navigation menu. This change is important because it provides clarity and transparency about the use of our resources, ensuring you have all the information you need at your fingertips (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-2fcc4ca958e093e082d40a93ab14ba804b31822d.md).

We also took a fresh approach to our content structure by collapsing sidebar categories for better clarity. This should make it easier for you to navigate through our documentation and find exactly what you’re looking for without feeling overwhelmed (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md). Additionally, we’ve synchronized our published strategies for Fabric adoption, which means you’ll have the latest methodologies at your disposal to guide your journey (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-9993fe2ef400b1868a1874a379e6fe864b68fd0c.md).

I want to take a moment to thank our contributors and the community for their ongoing support and feedback. Your input is invaluable and helps us create a more user-friendly experience for everyone. I hope these updates make your exploration of the Fabric Adoption Framework even more enjoyable! ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made a significant change to our documentation structure that I believe will enhance your experience as you navigate through the Fabric Adoption Framework. We decided to remove the "What's New" section entirely. While this may seem like a big shift, it’s all about streamlining our content and making it easier for you to find the information that truly matters. You can read more about this change in detail [here](https://github.com/TheTrustedAdvisor/FabricAdoptionFramework/commits/main?since=2025-06-01&until=2025-06-30).

Why does this matter? By eliminating the "What's New" section, we aim to reduce clutter and focus on providing you with clear, relevant, and actionable content. This change reflects our commitment to a more cohesive and user-friendly documentation experience. We want you to spend less time searching and more time implementing the insights you find here. 

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and community for their ongoing support and feedback, which has guided us in making these decisions. Your input is invaluable, and we hope these changes will create a more enjoyable and efficient journey for everyone involved in the Fabric Adoption Framework.
