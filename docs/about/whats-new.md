Lastly, we’ve made some structural adjustments, like collapsing sidebar categories for clarity, which should help streamline your navigation experience (Read more ›). I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and the community for their ongoing support and collaboration in making these updates possible. Your feedback is invaluable to us, and I can’t wait for you to explore all the new content we have in store! ## June 2025

Hello dear readers! This month, we made some significant changes to our documentation structure that I’m excited to share with you. We’ve decided to remove the “What’s New” section from our documentation. While it was a great way to keep everyone updated, we felt it was time to streamline our content and make navigation a bit more intuitive for you. This change allows us to focus on delivering the most relevant information without redundancy (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

By simplifying the structure, we hope you’ll find it easier to locate the information you need without sifting through multiple sections. Your input is always welcome, and I encourage you to share your thoughts as we continue to evolve together. Thank you for being part of our community!
