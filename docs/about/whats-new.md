# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve made significant strides in our documentation to ensure it’s clearer and more accessible. For instance, we’ve added a new License section to our documentation, along with a direct link in the navigation menu. This change not only helps clarify usage rights but also fosters transparency, which is essential for building trust within our community (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-2fcc4ca958e093e082d40a93ab14ba804b31822d.md).

We’ve also streamlined our sidebar categories to make navigation easier and more intuitive. By collapsing some categories, we’ve created a cleaner layout that allows you to find the information you need without feeling overwhelmed (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md). Additionally, we’ve synchronized our published strategies for Fabric adoption, ensuring that you have the most up-to-date resources at your fingertips (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-9993fe2ef400b1868a1874a379e6fe864b68fd0c.md).

These updates reflect our commitment to improving your journey with us, and I’m incredibly grateful to all the contributors who helped make this happen. I hope these changes make your experience more enjoyable and productive. Thank you for being part of our community! (531 commits)

## June 2025

Hello everyone! I’m excited to share some updates from June that reflect our commitment to making your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework even more user-friendly. This month, we made a significant change by removing the "What's New" documentation section. While it might seem like a small adjustment, this decision was made to streamline our content and help you find the information you need more efficiently. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

By simplifying our navigation and focusing on the most relevant content, we hope to enhance your journey through our documentation. This change is all about ensuring that you can quickly access the resources that matter most to you without the distraction of outdated sections. We believe that this will make your experience smoother and more enjoyable as you explore our framework. 

I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to everyone in our community who contributed to this decision. Your feedback is invaluable, and it truly helps us shape the best possible experience for all our users. I’m looking forward to seeing how these changes positively impact your engagement with our documentation!
