Hello, dear readers! This June, we made a significant change to our documentation structure that I believe will enhance your experience navigating our content. We’ve decided to remove the "What's New" section from our documentation. While this section served its purpose, we felt it was time to streamline our content and make room for more relevant information that directly supports your journey with the Fabric Adoption Framework. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).
