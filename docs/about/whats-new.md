## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we’ve made a significant change that I’d like to share with you. We’ve decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section from our site. This decision was made to streamline our content and make it easier for you to find the information that truly matters. By eliminating this section, we’re hoping to enhance your navigation experience and ensure that you can focus on the most relevant resources without any distractions (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

I understand that changes can sometimes feel a bit unsettling, but I genuinely believe this will lead to a more cohesive experience as you explore our documentation. By simplifying our structure, we’re aiming to create a more intuitive path for you to follow, allowing you to dive deeper into the content that supports your journey with the Fabric Adoption Framework. I want to take a moment to thank everyone in our community who contributed to this decision; your feedback is invaluable (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-3a35144aa92198574f983f6a3054dd2b5db3ab9c.md).

As we move forward, I’m excited to see how these changes will positively impact your experience. We’re committed to continuously improving our documentation, and I hope this new direction resonates with you. Thank you for being a part of our community, and I can’t wait to hear your thoughts on these updates!
