## June 2025

Hello everyone! I’m excited to share some updates from June that reflect our ongoing commitment to making the Fabric Adoption Framework documentation more user-friendly. This month, we made the significant decision to remove the "What's New" section from our documentation. While it was a helpful feature, we felt that consolidating information would streamline your experience and make it easier to find the content you need. You can read more about this change [here](https://www.fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This change matters because it allows us to focus on delivering more relevant and impactful content directly within the sections you visit most. By eliminating the redundancy of a separate "What's New" section, we hope to enhance your navigation experience and ensure that you’re always accessing the most current information without any unnecessary clicks. I truly believe this will make your journey through our documentation smoother and more intuitive.

I want to take a moment to thank our contributors and the community for their valuable feedback, which guided us in making this decision. Your insights are what help us grow and improve. As we continue to evolve, I’m looking forward to seeing how these changes positively impact your experience with our documentation. Let’s keep moving forward together!
