# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I'm excited to share some fresh updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we've made significant strides in organizing our content to make it more user-friendly. For instance, we collapsed sidebar categories for clarity, allowing you to navigate our resources more intuitively (Read more ›). Additionally, we added a License section to our documentation, which not only clarifies usage rights but also fosters transparency and trust within our community (Read more ›).

This means you'll have easier access to the information you need to effectively adopt Fabric in your projects (Read more ›). These changes are designed to empower you with the knowledge and tools necessary for successful implementation, and I hope they make your journey smoother and more enjoyable.

Lastly, I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to everyone who contributed to these updates. Your efforts are invaluable in shaping the Fabric Adoption Framework into a resource that truly serves our community. I'm looking forward to seeing how these enhancements impact your work!
