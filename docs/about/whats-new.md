## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we've made some significant changes to our documentation that I believe will enhance your experience as you navigate the Fabric Adoption Framework. Notably, we've decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section. While it was a space for updates, we felt that it was no longer serving its purpose effectively. Instead, we aim to integrate updates more seamlessly throughout the documentation, making it easier for you to find relevant information without having to check a separate section (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

We believe this will foster a more engaging and user-friendly environment for everyone involved.
