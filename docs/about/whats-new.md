## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we've made a significant change to our documentation that I want to share with you. We've decided to remove the "What's New" section from our site. This decision was made to streamline our content and make it easier for you to find the information you need without sifting through updates that may no longer be relevant. You can read more about this change [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

Why does this matter? Well, we believe that by simplifying our navigation and focusing on the most pertinent content, we can enhance your experience and help you engage more deeply with the resources we offer. We want you to spend less time searching and more time learning and implementing what you find. This change reflects our commitment to continuous improvement and responsiveness to your needs.

I want to take a moment to thank all our contributors and the community for your ongoing support and feedback. It’s your insights that guide us in making these important decisions. Together, we’re creating a more user-friendly environment that fosters growth and understanding. I’m excited to see how these changes will positively impact your journey with us!
