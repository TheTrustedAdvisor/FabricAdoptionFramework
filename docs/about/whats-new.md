## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I'm excited to share some wonderful updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we focused on refining our content and navigation to make it easier for you to find what you need. One of the key changes was the addition of a License section to our documentation, which is now easily accessible through the navigation menu (Read more ›). This addition not only clarifies how you can use our resources but also fosters a sense of transparency and trust within our community.

We also made strides in organizing our documentation by collapsing sidebar categories for clarity (Read more ›). This change aims to streamline your journey through our materials, allowing you to navigate more intuitively. Additionally, we synchronized various strategy methodology documents and expanded the visibility of strategy categories in our docs (Read more ›). These updates are designed to provide you with a more cohesive understanding of our strategies and their applications, making it easier for you to adopt and implement them effectively.

I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to our contributors and the community for your ongoing support and engagement. Your feedback helps us grow and improve, ensuring that our documentation remains relevant and user-friendly. I hope these changes make your experience even more enjoyable and impactful as you explore the Fabric Adoption Framework. (531 commits)

## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we made a significant change that I believe will enhance your experience on the Fabric Adoption Framework website. We decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section. This was a tough choice, but it allows us to streamline our content and focus on providing you with the most relevant and engaging information. You can read more about this decision in detail here: [Changes – 2025-06-03: Remove "What's New" Documentation Section (5a7d4f7)](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

Why does this matter? By simplifying our documentation structure, we aim to make it easier for you to find the information you need without sifting through less relevant updates. We hope this change will help you navigate the site more intuitively and focus on the content that truly matters to your adoption journey. I want to take a moment to thank everyone in our community for their feedback and support, which has been invaluable in guiding these decisions.

As we move forward, we expect this adjustment to foster a more cohesive and user-friendly environment. Your continued engagement is what drives us to improve, and I can't wait to see how these changes positively impact your experience. Thank you for being a part of our journey!
