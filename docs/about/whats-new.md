## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made a significant change to our documentation structure that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. We have decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section entirely. This decision was not made lightly, but we felt it was time to streamline our content and make it easier for you to find the information you truly need without sifting through updates that may not be relevant to your journey. If you're curious about the specifics of this change, you can read more about it [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

Why does this matter? By eliminating the "What's New" section, we aim to create a more cohesive and user-friendly navigation experience. Our goal is to ensure that you can quickly access the most relevant content without distractions. We believe that this will allow you to focus more on the resources that truly support your adoption journey, making your time spent with us more productive and enjoyable.

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and the community for their input and support throughout this process. Your feedback is invaluable, and it helps us create a better experience for everyone. I’m excited to see how these changes will positively impact your exploration of the Fabric Adoption Framework. As always, we’re here for you, and we appreciate your continued engagement with our documentation!
