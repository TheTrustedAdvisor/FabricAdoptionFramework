Hello, dear readers! I'm excited to share some wonderful updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. Notably, we’ve collapsed sidebar categories for clarity, making it easier for you to find what you need without feeling overwhelmed (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md).

This change not only helps clarify usage rights but also reflects our commitment to transparency and accessibility (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-2fcc4ca958e093e082d40a93ab14ba804b31822d.md). A heartfelt thank you to everyone who contributed to these changes; your efforts truly make a difference. I’m looking forward to seeing how these improvements impact your experience! (531 commits)

Hello, dear readers! This June, I want to share some significant updates that have taken place on our Fabric Adoption Framework website.
