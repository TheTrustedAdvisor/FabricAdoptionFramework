# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I'm excited to share some wonderful updates from August that I believe will enhance your experience with our Fabric Adoption Framework documentation. This month, we’ve made significant strides in improving the structure and clarity of our content. One of the most notable changes is the addition of a dedicated License section, which you can now easily find in our navigation menu. This update not only clarifies usage rights but also fosters transparency, making it easier for you to understand how to engage with our resources (Read more ›).

This means you can now find what you’re looking for more quickly, without feeling overwhelmed by too many options. Additionally, we’ve synchronized our published strategy methodology documents, ensuring that you have access to the latest insights and practices in one cohesive space (Read more ›). These changes are all about making your journey smoother and more intuitive, and I truly hope they help you feel more connected to the framework.

I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to our contributors and the community for their ongoing support and feedback. Your input is invaluable, and it drives us to continually improve. I’m looking forward to seeing how these enhancements will impact your experience as you explore and adopt the Fabric framework (Read more ›). Together, we’re building something great! (531 commits)

## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made a significant change to our documentation structure by removing the "What's New" section. While this might seem like a small shift, it’s a step towards streamlining our content and making it easier for you to find the information that truly matters. We believe that by focusing on the core resources, we can enhance your overall experience and help you navigate our documentation more intuitively. If you’re curious about the specifics of this change, you can read more about it [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This decision was made with your needs in mind. We want to ensure that our documentation remains relevant and accessible, and we felt that the "What's New" section was no longer serving that purpose effectively. By simplifying our navigation, we hope to create a smoother journey for you as you explore the resources available. Your feedback has been invaluable in guiding these changes, and I want to extend a heartfelt thanks to everyone who contributed their thoughts and suggestions.

As we move forward, I’m optimistic that these adjustments will lead to a more cohesive and user-friendly experience. I truly appreciate your continued support and engagement with the Fabric Adoption Framework. Together, we’re building a space that reflects our shared goals and aspirations. For those interested in the details of this month’s updates, feel free to check out the full list of changes [here](https://github.com/TheTrustedAdvisor/FabricAdoptionFramework/commits/main?since=2025-06-01&until=2025-06-30).
