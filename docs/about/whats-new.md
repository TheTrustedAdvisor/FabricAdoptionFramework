# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates from August 2025 that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve made significant strides in organizing our content to make it more accessible and user-friendly. For instance, we collapsed sidebar categories for clarity, allowing you to navigate through our resources with greater ease (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md). Additionally, we’ve synchronized our documentation with the latest strategy methodology documents, ensuring you have the most up-to-date information at your fingertips (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-f359f7af9238c768ef15da3bd9b4a18af45e0d78.md).

Another exciting change is the addition of a License section and a corresponding link in the navigation menu, which not only clarifies our usage policies but also enhances transparency for all our users (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-2fcc4ca958e093e082d40a93ab14ba804b31822d.md) (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-5c7af7d42f049ba974347b811dbea587eedf7a00.md). These updates matter because they reflect our commitment to providing a clear and supportive environment for your journey in adopting Fabric. 

As we continue to evolve, I am grateful for the contributions from our community that have made these changes possible. Your feedback and involvement are invaluable, and I look forward to seeing how these enhancements impact your experience. Together, we are building a more robust framework for everyone!
