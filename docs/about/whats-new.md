# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some warm updates from the Fabric Adoption Framework this August. We've been busy enhancing our documentation to make it more user-friendly and accessible. One of the most significant changes is the addition of a License section to our documentation, along with a direct link in the navigation menu. This makes it easier for you to understand our usage terms and ensures transparency as you explore our resources (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-2fcc4ca958e093e082d40a93ab14ba804b31822d.md).

Additionally, we've synchronized our published strategy methodology documents and expanded the visibility of our strategy category in the documentation. This means you can now find relevant strategies more easily, helping you navigate your adoption journey with confidence (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-08-f359f7af9238c768ef15da3bd9b4a18af45e0d78.md). I believe these changes will significantly enhance your experience, making it simpler to access the information you need.

Lastly, we’ve made some adjustments to the sidebar by collapsing categories for clarity, which should help streamline your navigation through our content. I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to everyone who contributed to these updates. Your input and support make our community stronger and our resources better (Read more › /about/changes/2025-08-07-428a3d2df5e70d8c5e77d1111e8edbf72439302d.md). Together, we’re creating a more welcoming space for all who seek to adopt the Fabric framework. (531 commits)

## July 2025

Hello, dear readers! I'm excited to share some wonderful updates from July 2025 that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we took significant steps to lay a solid foundation for our documentation. We introduced the initial content for the adoption framework, which is designed to guide you through the process of integrating our solutions effectively (Read more › /about/changes/2025-07-20-539a4eb0953aed159d80b720e6c646c0252a5378.md). This new content is not just a collection of information; it’s crafted to be a helpful companion as you navigate your adoption journey.

In addition to adding new content, we also made the decision to remove some initial documentation files that were no longer relevant (Read more › /about/changes/2025-07-20-3948fa7bc9ab671af8690e6527e831adebbec1dc.md). This cleanup allows us to streamline the information you see, making it easier for you to find what you need without wading through outdated material. With these updates, we aim to create a more cohesive and user-friendly experience that truly reflects your needs and feedback.

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to everyone who contributed to these changes. Your insights and dedication are invaluable to us, and I’m looking forward to seeing how these updates will positively impact your journey with the Fabric Adoption Framework. ## June 2025

Hello everyone! I’m excited to share some updates from June that I think will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework documentation. This month, we made the significant decision to remove the "What's New" section from our documentation. While it might seem like a big change, we believe it will streamline your navigation and help you find the information you need more efficiently. You can read more about this change [here](https://github.com/TheTrustedAdvisor/FabricAdoptionFramework/commits/main?since=2025-06-01&until=2025-06-30).

This adjustment is all about clarity and focus. By eliminating the "What's New" section, we aim to reduce clutter and make way for more relevant content that directly supports your journey with our framework. We understand that keeping up with changes can sometimes feel overwhelming, and we hope that this change will create a more straightforward path for you to access the most important information. If you’re curious about the specifics of this update, you can find more details [here](https://github.com/TheTrustedAdvisor/FabricAdoptionFramework/commits/main?since=2025-06-01&until=2025-06-30).

Thank you for being a part of our community and for your continued support. Your feedback is invaluable, and it drives us to create a better experience for everyone involved. We’re looking forward to seeing how these changes positively impact your use of the documentation. If you have any thoughts or suggestions, please don’t hesitate to reach out! (Read more ›)
