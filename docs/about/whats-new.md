## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we've made some significant updates to our Fabric Adoption Framework documentation that I believe will enhance your experience. Most notably, we've decided to remove the "What's New" section from our documentation. This change is part of our ongoing effort to streamline content and make it easier for you to find the information you need without sifting through outdated updates. You can read more about this decision [here](https://about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

By eliminating the "What's New" section, we're aiming for a more cohesive structure that allows you to navigate our resources with greater ease. This means you can spend less time searching and more time diving into the insights and tools that will support your journey. If you want to dive deeper into the reasoning behind this change, you can find more details [here](https://about/changes/2025-06-03-3a35144aa92198574f983f6a3054dd2b5db3ab9c.md).
