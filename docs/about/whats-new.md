# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates from August 2025 that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve made significant strides in refining our documentation. One of the highlights is the addition of a License section, which can now be easily accessed from the navigation menu. This change not only clarifies our licensing terms but also helps you understand your rights and responsibilities as you engage with our materials (Read more ›).

We also took a step back to improve the overall structure of our documentation. By collapsing sidebar categories, we aimed for a cleaner, more intuitive navigation experience. This means you can find what you need more quickly and with less clutter, making your journey through our resources smoother and more enjoyable (Read more ›). Additionally, we synchronized new content and updated our published strategy documents, ensuring that you have the most relevant and up-to-date information at your fingertips (Read more ›).

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to all the contributors and the community for their ongoing support and collaboration. Your feedback and dedication are what make this project thrive. I’m looking forward to seeing how these changes will positively impact your navigation and understanding of the Fabric Adoption Framework! ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we made some significant changes to our documentation that I believe will enhance your experience. We decided to remove the "What's New" section from our site. While it served its purpose in keeping you updated, we felt it was time to streamline our content and make navigation more intuitive for you. This change means you’ll find information more seamlessly integrated throughout the documentation rather than in a separate section (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

Why does this matter? By eliminating the "What's New" section, we aim to create a more cohesive reading experience. We want you to feel that every page you visit is up-to-date and relevant, without needing to check a separate area. Our goal is to make your journey through the documentation smoother and more enjoyable, and we believe this adjustment will help achieve that (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-3a35144aa92198574f983f6a3054dd2b5db3ab9c.md).

I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors for their ongoing support and feedback. Your insights help us continuously improve, and I’m excited about the positive impact these changes will have on your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. Together, we’re building something wonderful!
