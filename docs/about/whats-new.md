## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we've made some significant changes to the Fabric Adoption Framework documentation that I’m excited to share with you. One of the most notable updates is the removal of the "What's New" documentation section. While it served its purpose, we felt it was time to streamline our content and enhance your experience. This change means you'll find a more cohesive narrative throughout the documentation, making it easier to navigate and find the information you need (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

By eliminating the "What's New" section, we aim to create a more integrated approach to our documentation, allowing you to discover updates and changes within the context of the content itself. This should make your journey through our resources feel more natural and engaging. I truly believe this will enhance your learning experience and help you feel more connected to the material (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-3a35144aa92198574f983f6a3054dd2b5db3ab9c.md).
