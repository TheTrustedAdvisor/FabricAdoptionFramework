Hello, dear readers! This month, we made a significant change to our documentation structure by removing the "What's New" section. I know this might come as a surprise, but we felt it was time to streamline our content and make it easier for you to find the information you need without any extra clutter. You can read more about this decision and its implications in the detailed notes [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).
