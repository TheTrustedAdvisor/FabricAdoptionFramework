## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made a significant change to our documentation structure that I want to share with you. We decided to remove the "What's New" section from our site. While it was a space where we highlighted updates, we found that it was becoming less relevant as our content evolved. By streamlining our documentation, we hope to create a more cohesive experience for you, allowing you to focus on the information that truly matters. If you're curious about the details of this change, you can read more here: [Remove "What's New" Documentation Section](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This decision is all about enhancing your journey through our resources. By eliminating this section, we aim to declutter the navigation and make it easier for you to find what you're looking for without unnecessary distractions. We believe that this will lead to a more intuitive user experience, helping you engage more deeply with the content that supports your adoption of our framework. 

I want to take a moment to thank everyone in our community who has contributed to this evolution. Your feedback is invaluable, and it drives us to continually improve. We’re excited about the impact this change will have, and we look forward to hearing how it enhances your experience with our documentation.
