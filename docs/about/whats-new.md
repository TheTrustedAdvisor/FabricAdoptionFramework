# What's new

## August 2025

Hello, dear readers! I’m excited to share some wonderful updates from August 2025 that I believe will enhance your experience with the Fabric Adoption Framework. This month, we’ve made significant strides in improving our documentation and navigation. Notably, we’ve added a new License section to our documentation, along with a direct link in the navigation menu, making it easier for you to find essential information about our project’s licensing (Read more ›). Additionally, we’ve synchronized our published strategy methodology documents and expanded the visibility of the strategy category within our docs (Read more ›). These changes aim to provide you with clearer guidance and resources as you navigate your adoption journey.

We also took a thoughtful approach to our sidebar navigation by collapsing categories for better clarity. This should help you find what you need without feeling overwhelmed (Read more ›). I truly believe these adjustments will create a more user-friendly environment, allowing you to focus on what matters most—your adoption strategies. I want to extend my heartfelt thanks to our contributors and the community for their invaluable input and support in making these enhancements possible.

As we continue to grow and evolve, I’m eager to see how these changes impact your experience and help you on your journey with the Fabric Adoption Framework. Your feedback is always welcome, and I look forward to hearing how these updates resonate with you. ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This month, we made a significant change by removing the "What's New" documentation section from our website. While it may seem like a small adjustment, I believe it will help streamline your experience and make it easier to find the information you truly need. By consolidating our content, we hope to create a more cohesive and user-friendly navigation experience for everyone. If you're curious about the specifics of this change, you can check out the details here: [Read more ›](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

This decision was made with you in mind. We want to ensure that our documentation remains relevant and accessible, allowing you to focus on what matters most—adopting and implementing the Fabric framework effectively. By removing the "What's New" section, we aim to eliminate any confusion and enhance your overall experience. I truly appreciate the feedback from our community that led us to this decision, and I’m excited to see how it will positively impact your journey with us.
