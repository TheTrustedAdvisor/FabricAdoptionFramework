Hello, dear readers! This June, I want to share some significant changes we've made to our Fabric Adoption Framework documentation that I believe will enhance your experience. We've decided to remove the "What's New" documentation section entirely. This decision was made to streamline our content and ensure that you can find the information you need more easily. By consolidating our updates, we aim to create a more cohesive and user-friendly navigation experience (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

Our goal is to make your journey through our documentation smoother and more intuitive, allowing you to spend less time searching and more time learning. Thank you to our community for your ongoing feedback and support that has guided us in making these important decisions (Read more › /about/changes/2025-06-03-3a35144aa92198574f983f6a3054dd2b5db3ab9c.md).
