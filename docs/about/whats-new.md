Hello, dear readers! August has been a busy month for us at the Fabric Adoption Framework, and I'm excited to share some meaningful updates that enhance your experience with our documentation. One of the most notable changes is the addition of a new License section, which now has its own link in the navigation menu. This makes it easier for you to access important licensing information at any time (Read more ›). We believe that clarity around licensing is essential for your understanding and compliance, and we hope this change helps streamline your journey.

This small adjustment can make a big difference in how you find the information you need. Additionally, we've synchronized various strategy methodology documents and expanded the visibility of our strategy categories, ensuring that you have access to the latest insights and resources for effective Fabric adoption (Read more ›).

I want to take a moment to thank our contributors and the community for their invaluable input and support. ## June 2025

Hello, dear readers! This June, we've made some significant changes to our documentation that I believe will enhance your experience. Most notably, we've decided to remove the "What's New" section from our documentation. This change was made to streamline our content and focus on providing you with the most relevant information without any redundancy. You can read more about this decision [here](https://fabricadoptionframework.com/about/changes/2025-06-03-5a7d4f72ccbbd73c700b77c1b485216d1e29c0ea.md).

I understand that change can sometimes feel unsettling, but this adjustment is all about clarity and ease of navigation. By removing the "What's New" section, we aim to declutter your journey through our resources, allowing you to find what you need more intuitively. Our goal is to ensure that you can focus on adopting the framework effectively and efficiently, without unnecessary distractions. 

I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to our contributors and community for their ongoing support and feedback, which guide us in making these important decisions. We believe this change will positively impact your experience with our documentation, and we are excited to continue evolving together.
